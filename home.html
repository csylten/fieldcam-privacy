Built for documentation work

For service technicians, inspectors and project teams who need photos that explain themselves, with the facts printed into the image.

  • Work apart from personalJob photos live in FieldCam's own library, not in your camera roll. Turn off Save to Photos and they never mix with family pictures.
  • Time, GPS and addressStamped automatically in an overlay style you choose, with your own logo.
  • Tags and notesAdd component codes as you shoot, scan labels, and create numbered series such as GA101 to GA130. Call them tags, components or assets.
  • Before & AfterTwo photos of the same job in one clean image. Name the sides as you like, for example Reference and Actual.
  • PDF reports with templatesPick pictures, add an explanation under each, and send one report. Save the look as a template with your colors, header and footer, and share it as a file.
  • Draw, crop and rotateArrows, boxes, circles and text on a photo, and crop or straighten without touching the original capture.
  • Video toolsTime-lapse with an interval of your choice, and a stopwatch with laps printed into the video.
  • Team TemplatesShare your camera and overlay setup with a QR code, so a whole crew documents the same way.
  • Backup you controlCopy to your own iCloud Drive or a folder you choose. There is no FieldCam server.

Private by design

No user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your photos, videos and location stay on your device and in the backup destination you pick, and with Save to Photos off your camera roll never sees a work picture. Only the street address and the map use Apple's services. This website sets no cookies and does no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
