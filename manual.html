Words Used in FieldCam

  • Tag: a saved label, such as a component ID like GA101. Tags are printed on the photo and can be searched in the Library. You can call them something else, such as Component or Asset, in Settings → Storage & Tagging → Name for Tags. The word is shared in a Team Template.
  • Place: your own text for where a photo was taken, for example “Pump room 3”.
  • Notes: a free description of the job or what the photo shows.
  • Location: the GPS position, as coordinates and an address.
  • Overlay: the information printed on the photo: time, date, location, place, tags, notes and logo.
  • Library: FieldCam’s own collection of photos and videos. It is separate from the Photos app.

Take Photos and Video

Basics

  • Switch between Photo and Video with the toggle at the top of the screen.
  • The flip button next to the flash switches between the back and front camera.
  • The button at the left of the zoom row switches between the normal picture and a square 1:1 picture.
  • On iPhones with a Camera Control button, that button also takes the picture.

Zoom

  • Tap 0.5, 1, 2 or 4 to jump to that zoom. Only the zoom levels your camera has are shown.
  • Drag sideways over the zoom buttons to open the zoom ruler and zoom smoothly. The whole range is covered in less than one screen width.
  • You can also pinch the picture to zoom.

Focus, flash and lock

  • Tap the picture to focus.
  • The padlock at the top right can lock the lens and the focus, so they stay put during a whole job.
  • The flash menu at the top left offers Off, Auto, On and Torch (constant light), depending on your camera.
  • A grid and a level can be switched on in Settings → Photo. They help you frame the shot and are never saved in the file.

Tags and Notes

The tag button

  • The tag button next to the shutter opens Tags & Notes. A preview at the top shows how the overlay will look on the photo.
  • Place: type where you are. Your recent places are offered as quick buttons.
  • Tags: search your saved tags or type a new one. Tap a tag in the list to change it for this photo, and drag to reorder.
  • Notes: type a description. Notes are printed on the photo and can be searched later.

Faster tagging

  • Tap the scan button in the tag field to scan a QR code or barcode on a label. The code becomes the tag.
  • Type just a number, such as 104, and FieldCam offers the next tag in the series you were using, for example GA104.
  • Choose “Add a range” to create a whole run of tags at once, for example GA101 to GA115. They are added to your tag library, not to the current photo.
  • “Clear all” empties the place, tags and notes for the next capture.

When to tag

  • Settings → Storage & Tagging → Tag Photos decides the order: tag before taking the photo, after taking it, or automatically (ask only when no tag is selected).

Overlay and Logo

Settings → Watermark & Overlay

  • Style: choose from samples drawn with your own colours, font and logo.
  • Appearance: position, colours, text size and font.
  • Time & Date: switch them on and choose the format.
  • Location: Off, saved in the file only (searchable, not visible), or printed on the photo as coordinates, address, or both.
  • Logo: choose one from Photos, from Files, or from a link. A logo from a link can be shared with your team in a Team Template.

Changing it afterwards

  • Open a photo, tap ··· and choose Edit Tags & Notes to correct the place, tags or notes printed on it. Time, date and position can’t be changed.
  • This needs “Allow Editing Tags Later” in Settings → Storage & Tagging, which keeps an untouched copy of each photo.

Video, Time-Lapse and Stopwatch

Settings → Video

  • Resolution and frame rate: only frame rates your camera supports are listed.
  • Record Audio: music from other apps keeps playing, and the microphone is only used while you record.
  • Capture Photo While Recording adds a second shutter button for stills during a recording.

Time-lapse

  • Time-lapse saves one frame per interval (1 to 60 seconds) and plays them back at 30 frames per second, without sound.
  • Keep FieldCam open on screen while it records.

Stopwatch

  • With the stopwatch on, Start/Stop, Lap and Reset buttons appear at the right edge, also before you start recording.
  • The time and the laps are printed in the video.

Library

Find and view

  • Open the Library with the album button at the bottom left of the camera.
  • Search tags, place, address and notes, or filter by date and tag. “Untagged” shows photos without tags.
  • Tap a picture to open it. Swipe to move between pictures. Tap a photo for full screen, and pinch to zoom. Turn the phone for landscape.

The ··· menu on a photo

  • Info: camera details, position on a map and the tags and notes.
  • Edit Tags & Notes: change place, notes and tags.
  • Draw & Annotate: add lines, arrows, boxes, circles, drawings, text and marker.
  • Crop & Rotate: reframe and straighten.
  • Before & After, Share, Export PDF Report and Delete.

Several at once

  • Tap Select, tick the pictures, then Share, Report or Delete.

Before & After

Make one

  • Open the first photo, tap ···, then Before & After.
  • Take After Photo opens the camera with this photo’s tags and a guide for matching the angle. Tap the eye in the banner to fade the before photo over the viewfinder.
  • Choose From Library lets you pick the second photo. Photos with the same tags are listed first.

Adjust and save

  • Change style, direction, labels and the info band under the preview. Touch and hold a photo to zoom and move it.
  • Tap a photo to rename its label, for example Reference and Actual. Settings → Before & After → Labels has ready-made pairs and lets you save your own. In More Options, “Use These Settings as Default” keeps your wording for new images, and a Team Template carries it to your team.
  • “Delete originals” removes the two source photos after saving. The image can then no longer be re-styled.
  • Your default look is set in Settings → Before & After.

PDF Reports

Make a report

  • In the Library, tap Select, choose the pictures and tap Report. Choose New Report, or Add to Existing Report.
  • Fill in the title, project and who prepared it, and an introduction if you like. A title is suggested from the tags on the pictures.
  • Choose paper size (A4, A3, A5, Letter or Legal), picture quality, one or two pictures per page, cover page and logo.
  • Choose what is printed with each picture: date and time, tags, GPS position, address and place, file name.
  • Write an explanation under each picture. If you leave it empty, nothing is printed. Tap Reorder to change the order, and swipe a picture to leave it out.
  • Create makes the PDF and opens the share sheet, so you can send it straight away.

Come back to it

  • Every report you create is saved. Open the Reports row at the top of the Library to change it and create a new PDF.
  • “Save Report for Later” keeps your setup without creating a PDF.

Storage and Backup

Storage

  • FieldCam keeps its own library inside the app. If the app is deleted, that library is deleted with it.
  • Save to Photos also puts a copy in your Photos library, which stays. You can also save a copy without the overlay.

Backup

  • Settings → Backup copies new photos and videos to iCloud or to any folder in the Files app, such as Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive.
  • It is a one-way safety copy: it never deletes or changes anything at the destination. Use Back Up Now to catch up.

File names

  • Settings → Storage & Tagging → File Names builds the file name from a prefix, date and time, a running number and the first tag or note.

Team Template

Share your setup

  • Settings → Team Template → Save Current Settings as Template keeps your camera, overlay and logo layout settings so you can switch back to them.
  • Share as QR Code gives your teammates the same look. You can include your saved tags, and a logo link so the logo downloads automatically.

Use a shared setup

  • Open Settings → Team Template → Import Team Template and scan the QR code, or import a saved copy from Photos or Files.
  • FieldCam shows what is included before you apply it. Applying replaces your current camera, overlay and logo layout settings.
