FieldCam Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests for FieldCam — the field-documentation camera app.

Email csylten@gmail.com

Before you write in

  • Include your device model and iOS version — Settings → General → About.
  • Include the FieldCam version — Settings → the version number shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.
  • If something crashed, a screenshot or a short description of what you were doing helps a lot.

Common questions

Where are my photos stored? On your device, inside FieldCam. Turn on “Save to Photos” in Settings to also keep a copy in your device's Photos app, and turn on Backup to copy captures to your own iCloud Drive or a folder you choose.

Does FieldCam need an internet connection? No — capturing, tagging, editing and exporting all work fully offline. A connection is only used if you add a logo from a link, or use iCloud backup.

Is my data sent anywhere? No. See the privacy policy for details.
