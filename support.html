FieldCam Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests for FieldCam, the field-documentation camera app.

Email csylten@gmail.com Read the User Guide

Before you write in

  • Include your device model and iOS version: Settings → General → About on your iPhone.
  • Include the FieldCam version: in FieldCam, Settings → About FieldCam.
  • If something crashed, a screenshot or a short description of what you were doing helps a lot.

Common questions

Where are my photos stored? On your device, inside FieldCam. Turn on “Save to Photos” in Settings → Storage & Tagging to also keep a copy in your device's Photos app, and turn on Backup to copy captures to your own iCloud Drive or a folder you choose.

Does FieldCam need an internet connection? No. Capturing, tagging, editing and exporting all work fully offline. A connection is only used if you add a logo from a link, or use iCloud backup.

Can I call tags something else? Yes. In Settings → Storage & Tagging → Name for Tags you can choose Component, Asset, Equipment or your own word.

Can I rename Before and After? Yes. Tap a photo in the Before & After editor and type your own word, for example Reference and Actual.

Is my data sent anywhere? No. See the privacy policy for details.
